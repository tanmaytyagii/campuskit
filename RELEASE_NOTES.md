# CampusKit 6.0.0-rc.1 release notes

**Release candidate, 31 October 2026.** For students and teachers trying the new version before the final 6.0.0. The full list of changes is in `CHANGELOG.md`; test results are in `QA_REPORT.md`.

## What's new

**Your own projects, not just templates.** My projects now holds the projects you're actually building:

- give each one a status, tags and the technology it uses;
- add milestones with due dates;
- keep notes, which are saved as you type;
- keep links, and see a timeline of what changed.

Pin the ones you're working on, archive the ones you've finished, and search across all of them.

**A README and a viva sheet, drafted from your project.** On a project's page, choose "README and viva sheet". CampusKit drafts both from what you've entered and marks every part it couldn't fill as "To write", so nothing is made up. Download as Word or Markdown, or print to PDF.

**A dashboard.** `/dashboard` shows your deadlines for the next two weeks, what changed recently, pages you visited, and viva questions due for review.

**Go anywhere with the keyboard.** Press Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) to jump to any page, template, document or project of your own. Press `?` for every shortcut.

**Mock vivas and viva progress.** A mock viva asks 8 questions across topics, with an optional timer. `/viva/progress` shows how you rated yourself by topic and which topics need work. It counts only your own ratings: opening a question never counts as knowing it.

**Works offline, and installs like an app.** After one visit, your projects, the dashboard and viva practice work without a connection.

**An optional AI assistant.** `/assistant` can explain a template's architecture, your code or an error, suggest viva questions, plan what to learn, or review your documentation. It uses your own Anthropic API key, which is kept only in this tab and never saved. You see exactly what will be sent before anything leaves your browser. Answers are labelled as written by AI; check them against your code.

**Hindi menus.** Choose हिन्दी in Settings to see the menus, dashboard, command palette and shortcut list in Hindi.

**A welcome guide and settings.** New students get three short, skippable questions and template suggestions with the reason for each. Settings holds your profile, theme, language and offline storage. It also lets you delete everything CampusKit saved in your browser.

## Upgrading from 5.0

Nothing to do. Your saved progress, report drafts and viva ratings carry over. Backup files made by 5.0 still restore. New backups also include your projects and profile.

## Please note

- **This is a release candidate.**
  - The Hindi text was drafted with AI help and hasn't been checked by a native speaker yet; corrections are welcome.
  - The AI assistant was tested against a simulated service, not the live one.
- Only the menus, dashboard, palette and shortcut list are translated. Template guides, viva questions, demos and documentation are still in English.
- Using the AI assistant costs money on your own Anthropic account and needs a connection. Everything else is free and works offline.
- Templates are a starting point: follow your institution's rules, and make sure you can explain everything you submit.
