# Code of conduct

CampusKit is built for students, and many contributors are students too. Everyone should be able to ask questions, make mistakes and learn here without being made to feel small.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming to newcomers. A beginner's question deserves a patient, specific answer.
- Be respectful when you disagree. Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Give credit. Say where an idea or a piece of code came from.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming you understood someone.
- Keep discussions on topic and in public channels, unless they involve personal information.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or sexual language and images.
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to harass someone.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as their address, phone number or student details, without permission.
- Helping or encouraging anyone to break their institution's academic integrity rules, for example by submitting work they did not do.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and in any other space where you represent CampusKit.

## Reporting a problem

Contact the maintainers privately through the contact details on the repository's profile page, or, for security issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Say what happened, where and when, and include links or screenshots if you can. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behaviour breaks this code of conduct. They will explain their decision to the person involved, unless doing so would put someone at risk.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
